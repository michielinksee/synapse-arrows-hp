What is happening

AI descriptions contradict official information

When a customer asks an AI about a shop, the address, branches, prices or reservation rules it returns can differ from the official website. We measured it.

12 of 20restaurants had contradictions

In October 2026 we took the 20 Sapporo restaurants that four AIs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) named most often, extracted 142 factual statements from their answers and checked each against the restaurant's official website or official social accounts. 18 statements contradicted the official information; 29 could not be verified because the official sources do not state them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AI映え診断 (the AI visibility check)?
A service by Synapse Arrows Pte. Ltd. that checks how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity describe a shop or company, finds where their answers contradict the official information, and fixes it. Two free stages (Light and the detailed check) are followed by paid options: a fact-check report, monthly monitoring and an implementation retainer. The check itself is in Japanese and aimed at businesses in Japan.
What do the free stages show?
Light (no sign-up) looks at the website itself: whether AI crawlers can read it, whether it links to the directories each AI relies on for that industry, and whether the name, address, hours and menu are machine-readable. The detailed check (email confirmation required) asks the four AIs three fixed questions once and reports whether you were named, how many statements were wrong and which sources were cited. One wrong statement is shown in full; the rest are checked in the paid report.
AI answers change from day to day. How do you handle that?
We never judge from a single answer. The paid fact-check report asks 20–30 questions tailored to your industry and area, three times each on all four AIs, and checks every repeated wrong statement against the official information. Monthly monitoring repeats the same questions every month and reports the change in numbers.
Do you guarantee rankings or recommendations?
No. What we change is the number of wrong addresses, prices, opening hours and menu items AI gives when it describes you, and the consistency of the listings AI relies on. The effect is shown by re-measurement.
Are prices per shop?
Yes. All prices are per shop or company, in Japanese yen and excluding tax. For several shops or a whole company we quote individually, and for clients in Singapore we quote in SGD on request.
Why do monthly plans run for at least six months?
The listings AI relies on take time to refresh, and seeing the effect of a fix in numbers takes months. If you move from a one-off report to a monthly plan within a month, the report fee is deducted from the first month.
How does KanseiLINK relate to Synapse Arrows?
KanseiLINK is a third-party instrument operated by Synapse Arrows that measures how AI agents can discover, connect to and use software. The AI visibility check runs on KanseiLINK's server, but consultations, orders and paid work go through Synapse Arrows. KanseiLINK ratings do not change with any contract.
