AI visibility tools for the Japanese market: 12 compared, six from Japan and six from overseas (2026)

10 November 2026 · Synapse Arrows · Vendor pages checked 5 November 2026

About this article: Written by Synapse Arrows, which sells AI visibility work and whose own service, AI映え診断, is one of the twelve tools below. For that reason the selection criteria come first and every tool is described on the same items. Features and prices are taken from each vendor's own site as it stood on 5 November 2026, in the currency the vendor uses, and will change; check the vendor's page before deciding. Nothing here is a ranking.

An AI visibility tool shows how often, and how, generative AI such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews mentions, cites or recommends a brand. For a company that sells in Japan, or is entering Japan, the question is narrower than "which tool is best": which tools put Japanese questions to the AIs and read Japanese sources, and which measure the English-language market. This article is the English version of our Japanese comparison, rewritten for that reader. Japanese tools are described in more depth because less has been written about them in English; the overseas tools are covered briefly.

1. What an AI visibility tool does

AI answers change from day to day. To avoid doing work and never finding out whether it changed anything, you need a tool that measures the current position and the change. The five things these tools do:

  1. Check whether the AIs mention or cite your brand, across several AIs.
  2. Show which keywords trigger Google AI Overviews and what they say.
  3. Compare your share of voice against competitors.
  4. Diagnose whether your own content is easy for AI to read and understand.
  5. Monitor third-party mentions (citations) over time.

2. How to choose

  • Which AIs it covers: ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Overviews, Perplexity, Copilot.
  • How it measures: is the method stated, and is web search on when the questions are asked?
  • Which markets and languages: can it put Japanese questions to the AIs and read Japanese sources? Can it also measure English? This is the item most often overlooked by companies on either side of the border.
  • Independence: is the measurement separate from whoever does the implementation, or can a third-party measurement be used alongside?
  • Price and entry: free plan or trial, and whether you can start small.
  • Operation: how often it updates and how a team uses it.

3. The six Japanese tools

1. AI映え診断 (Synapse Arrows), this site

Vendor: Synapse Arrows Pte. Ltd., Singapore, with a Tokyo base.

What it does: puts a fixed set of questions about a business to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity with web search on, extracts the factual claims the AIs make about the business, and checks each claim against the official website. The free Light check needs no sign-up; a free detailed check (four AIs, three fixed questions, once) needs email confirmation. The questions are built per industry from our own measurement of where each AI reads in Japan, so the check also tells you whether your site is connected to the platforms the AIs rely on for your industry. Works in Japanese and English, for Japanese shops and companies and for overseas companies addressing Japan.

Pricing (JPY, excluding tax, per shop or company): Light check and detailed check free; fact-check report JPY 50,000 one-off; report plus one re-measurement after a month JPY 80,000 one-off; monthly monitoring JPY 30,000/month with a 6-month minimum (then month by month). Prices set 2026-10-28. Prices are in Japanese yen. For clients in Singapore we quote in SGD on request.

Markets: Japanese and English. Independence: the measurement runs on KanseiLINK, an instrument operated by the same group that has no sales function of its own; Synapse Arrows, which sells the service, also offers implementation, so it is a party to the result. We say so here rather than claim neutrality for ourselves.

Source: synapsearrows.com/en/services/ai-aeo

2. MIERUCA GEO (ミエルカGEO), Faber Company

What it does: GEO and LLMO diagnosis and improvement. Visualises how your company and competitors are mentioned in AI search and which sources the AIs cite. Consulting can be combined with the tool, which suits a company that wants the vendor to act on the findings as well as report them.

Pricing: one site JPY 49,800/month; several sites from JPY 99,800/month; a separate set-up fee; an AI search share-measurement add-on from JPY 50,000/month (vendor site, 5 November 2026; the vendor notes prices may be revised).

Markets: English-language coverage: ask the vendor. Independence: the vendor's own tool, with the vendor also doing implementation.

3. AKARUMI, ipe

What it does: daily quantitative tracking of your brand's mention rate across the major AI models, together with AI crawler visits to your site. The crawler view is the distinctive part: it shows whether the AIs' bots are fetching your pages at all, which is the precondition for being cited.

Pricing: not published (enquire); 7-day free trial.

Markets: English-language coverage: ask the vendor. Independence: vendor's own tool.

4. Keywordmap, CINC

What it does: AI search analysis added to an established Japanese SEO analysis platform. Analyses the AIs' answers and the pages they cite alongside conventional keyword data, so a team that already runs Japanese SEO in Keywordmap can see AI citations in the same place.

Pricing: not published (enquire); 7-day free trial.

Markets: English-language coverage: ask the vendor. Independence: vendor's own tool.

5. DolphinX AIO, Media Reach (メディアリーチ)

What it does: visualises brand exposure and citations across several AIs. It publishes a full price list and states no set-up fee, which makes it easy to budget for from outside Japan.

Pricing: Light JPY 30,000, Pro JPY 50,000, Business JPY 100,000 per month, excluding tax, with discounts for six-month and annual contracts; no set-up fee; 14-day free trial (vendor site, 5 November 2026).

Markets: English-language coverage: ask the vendor. Independence: vendor's own tool.

6. Generative AI brand-mention checker (生成AIブランド言及チェッカー), Nyle (ナイル)

What it does: a quick check of whether the major generative AIs mention your brand. It is a simple checker rather than a monitoring platform, and a reasonable first step for a Japanese brand name before paying for anything.

Pricing: free (vendor site, 5 November 2026; confirm on the tool's own page).

Markets: English-language coverage: ask the vendor. Independence: a simple checker; not a third-party measurement service.

7. Frequently asked questions

Is there a free way to start measuring AI visibility in Japan?

Yes. Among the twelve, AI映え診断 has a free check with no sign-up, Nyle's brand-mention checker is free, and AthenaHQ's Essential plan is free; several others offer a 7- or 14-day trial. Start with a free check to learn where you stand, then decide whether a paid plan is needed.

Should a company entering Japan use a Japanese tool or an overseas tool?

Measure the Japanese market with a tool that puts Japanese questions to the AIs and reads Japanese sources, and measure the English-language market separately. Whether an overseas tool measures Japanese queries against Japanese platforms is something to confirm with the vendor; the Japanese tools' English-language coverage is likewise "ask the vendor" for most of them.

Is the agency's own measurement tool enough?

It tends to become self-grading. Measurement by a party separate from the one doing the implementation makes it easier to confirm whether anything actually changed.

Which AIs should we measure?

The ones your customers use. In Japan that is ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews; in English-speaking markets Perplexity and Copilot also matter. Our October 2026 research found that each AI reads different sources even for the same industry.

Will a tool by itself improve our AI visibility?

No. These tools measure. Improving the result takes primary information, structured data and third-party mentions in the target language; our guide for companies entering Japan sets out the steps.

8. Summary

Choose on three things: which AIs the tool covers, whether it measures the market and language you are entering, and whether the measurement is separate from the implementation. Start with a free check to learn where you stand, and measure Japan and the English-language market separately.
