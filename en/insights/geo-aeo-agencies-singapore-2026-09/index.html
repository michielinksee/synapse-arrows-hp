AEO and GEO agencies in Singapore (2026): 17 agencies compared on pricing and terms, from their own pages (checked October 2026)

30 September 2026 · updated 22 October 2026 · Synapse Arrows

About this article: Synapse Arrows wrote it. We also offer AI search services in Singapore. We are included in the table with the same columns as everyone else, but kept out of the purpose groups; see "About the operator". Agencies are grouped by purpose and are not ranked. Every fact comes from the agency's own website as it stood on 22 October 2026, with a source link. We did not use third-party rankings, review scores or self-reported results, and we left out guarantee claims.

Generative engine optimization (GEO), answer engine optimization (AEO) and AI SEO all describe the same goal: being understood, cited and recommended when people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews. Singapore agencies use different names for it, cover different AI engines and price it very differently. This page puts what each agency states on its own site side by side, so you can shortlist before you speak to anyone.

Key points as of 22 October 2026
  • Five of the 17 agencies publish prices. Monthly retainers start from about S$1,000–S$2,500 and run to S$8,000 or more.
  • Contract terms differ widely: cancel anytime (Stridec), monthly plans run for at least six months (Synapse Arrows), 12 months (Hashmeta AI).
  • Most agencies offer a free audit, report or consultation as the first step.
  • Most name ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews; Claude and Copilot are named by some.
  • Several mention grant support such as PSG, EDGE or EDG. Eligibility depends on your company.

Before you shortlist: four things to decide

  1. One-off or ongoing. An audit tells you where you stand; a retainer does the work and re-measures it.
  2. Which AI engines matter to your buyers. The engines covered differ by agency and sometimes by plan.
  3. How results are measured. Ask whether the same questions are re-run on a schedule, with web search on, and whether you will see the sources the AI relied on.
  4. Markets and languages. The sources AI relies on differ by language, so check whether the agency has measured your market.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GEO cost in Singapore?

Among agencies that publish prices on their own sites (checked on 22 October 2026), monthly GEO retainers start from about S$1,000–S$2,500 and run to S$8,000 or more: Kaizenaire from SGD 1,500, MediaPlus from about S$1,000 for an add-on and S$2,500 for a core programme, Stridec S$2,500 to S$8,000, and Hashmeta AI US$1,500 to US$10,000. Most agencies do not publish prices.

Is there a minimum contract?

It varies. Stridec states its plans can be cancelled at any time. Synapse Arrows' monthly plans run for six months, then month to month (its prices are published in yen; SGD on request). Hashmeta AI states a 12-month minimum. Agencies that do not publish pricing usually confirm terms in a proposal.

Can I get a free GEO audit in Singapore?

Yes. Most of the agencies listed offer a free audit, report or consultation, including OOm, MediaPlus, Verz Design, Pulse Digital, AI Studio, Stridec, Hashmeta AI and Synapse Arrows.

Are there grants for GEO or AI SEO?

Several agencies mention Singapore grant schemes on their sites, such as PSG (Impossible Marketing, Audience Intelligence, Verz Design), EDGE (MediaPlus, OOm, Impossible Marketing) and EDG (Verz Design). Stridec no longer mentions MRA on its pricing page. Eligibility depends on your company, so confirm with the agency and the scheme's administrator.

Does any agency guarantee that AI will recommend my brand?

No one can guarantee what an AI assistant will answer. This article leaves out guarantee claims. A sound engagement measures the same questions repeatedly across several AI engines and reports the change.
