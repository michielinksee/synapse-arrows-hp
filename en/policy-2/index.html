Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026 (added 1.4 AI visibility check, translated from the Japanese policy)

Synapse Arrows Pte. Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our products and services.

Products & services covered

This policy applies to all of our products:

  • KanseiLINK — AEO Ratings Platform (kansei-link.com)
  • ScaNavi — Japanese Alcohol Intelligence App (scanavi.app / iOS / Android)
  • Linksee Memory — Agent Memory MCP Server (open source)
  • Bantou — AI Automated Bookkeeping & Tax Classification Agent
  • CardWize — AI Card Management Platform
  • ScaNavi-PC — PC & Electronics Review Service
  • ScaNavi-Kaden — Home Electronics Intelligence Service
  • AI visibility check (AI映え診断) — a check of how ready a shop's or company's website is for AI (synapsearrows.com/ai-bae-shindan/)
  • Our consulting and advisory services

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

  • Email address (for authentication)
  • Name (optional)
  • Google / Apple account information (when using social login)
  • User preferences and settings

1.2 Usage data

  • Feature usage across our products
  • Search queries and scan history (ScaNavi)
  • AEO score lookup history (KanseiLINK)
  • Error logs and crash reports
  • Device information (OS, browser, device type)

1.3 Product-specific data

ScaNavi (mobile & web):

  • Sake label images captured by camera (processed by AI, not permanently stored on our servers)
  • Favorites and tasting notes
  • Location data (only with permission, for nearby store search)

KanseiLINK:

  • API usage logs via MCP server
  • Service search and report submission data

Bantou:

  • Transaction data from linked accounting SaaS (e.g., freee) via API
  • Classification results and correction history
  • Transaction data is stored on the accounting SaaS servers, not permanently on our servers

Linksee Memory:

  • Linksee Memory is a fully local, open-source tool. No user data is sent to our servers
  • We only collect anonymous usage statistics (npm download counts, etc.)

1.4 AI visibility check (AI映え診断, synapsearrows.com/ai-bae-shindan/)

This is how we handle the free check and applications for the detailed check. The check is processed and recorded on the KanseiLINK server we operate (mcp.kansei-link.com).

Free check:

  • What we collect: the website URL you enter and the result of the check (stored so that the link to the result can be opened later), and a value converted from your IP address (to prevent abuse; we do not store the IP address itself). We do not ask for your email address or any other information that identifies you.
  • We also record the industry you chose, the domain of the site you came from (which site you came from; we do not record the location of that page or search terms), the utm values in the link (utm_source, utm_campaign), and the page on our site where you started the check. This is to count which industries and which routes the check is used from, and it is kept for 1 year, the same as the result of the check.
  • When the check cannot be completed (for example, the site does not respond in time, cannot be found, or is a page on a listing site), we record only the host name of the address you entered (without the path or search terms), the type of failure, the industry you chose and where you came from, for 90 days, to find out which sites cannot be read.
  • The check reads only publicly available pages and robots.txt. We do not send information about your shop or company to AI services.
  • How long we keep it: the value converted from your IP address is deleted after 30 days, and the result of the check (including the URL) after 1 year.

Applications for the detailed check:

  • What we collect: your shop's or company's website URL, your email address, your answers to the questionnaire (industry, area, size, the problems you have, the tools you use, whether you want a consultation, and so on, including free text for "other" and for your city or town), your two consents (consent to the check and consent to aggregation), a value converted from your IP address with a secret key (it cannot be turned back into the original IP address, and we do not store the raw IP address), and the times of receipt and confirmation. We do not ask for your name, phone number or address.
  • We also record the domain of the site you came from (we do not record the location of that page or search terms), the utm values in the link (utm_source, utm_campaign), the page on our site where you started the application, and the number of your free check result (to count which routes applications come from). These are deleted after 180 days, the same as your email address.
  • Why we use it:
    • Your email address is used only to confirm your application and to tell you the result of the check. We do not use it to send announcements or newsletters.
    • The URL and your questionnaire answers are used as material for the detailed check.
    • Only if you consent to aggregation, we aggregate the questionnaire choices in a form that does not identify you or your shop or company.
    • The value converted from your IP address is used only to investigate abuse.
  • How long we keep it:
    • If you do not confirm your application through the link in the confirmation email, we delete everything after 7 days.
    • If you confirm it, we delete your email address, the URL, the value converted from your IP address and your free text 180 days after we send you the result (or after confirmation, if we have not sent it). If you did not consent to aggregation, we delete the whole application record.
    • To count how many emails are sent to the same address, we keep a value converted from the email address and the time of sending for only 2 days.
  • Service providers we entrust it to:
    • Google LLC (Google Workspace; United States): sending the confirmation email and the result email, from our address contact@synapse-arrows.com. We entrust your email address and the body of the emails.
    • Cloudflare, Inc. (Turnstile; United States): the check against automated mass applications. The Cloudflare check runs on the check and application screens, and your IP address and the check token are sent to Cloudflare for that check.
    • Four AI companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google [Gemini] and Perplexity): to see how AI answers about your shop or company in the detailed check, we send only the shop or company name, the area and the URL. We do not send your email address or questionnaire answers.
  • Based on the consent you give when you apply, we entrust the information to the providers above to the extent needed. To ask for deletion, contact us at the address below.

1.5 Request and consultation form (synapsearrows.com/contact/)

This covers what you send through our request and consultation form (including the English version at synapsearrows.com/en/contact/).

  • What we collect: company or business name, your name, email address, industry, website URL (optional), what you need (materials, free consultation, quote, or something else), your message (optional), and your agreement to this policy. We also record the time of sending, the page language, the page you sent it from, the referring page, and any utm values in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign).
  • To stop automated mass submissions, a Cloudflare check (Turnstile) runs on the form page, and your IP address is sent to Cloudflare for that check. The check's settings are loaded from the KanseiLINK server we operate (mcp.kansei-link.com); the contents of the form are not sent to that server. The form records do not keep your IP address.
  • Why we use it: only to send materials, reply to your enquiry, and prepare quotes. We do not use it for newsletters or bulk mailings.
  • How long we keep it: we delete it 2 years after our last exchange. If you want it deleted sooner, contact us at the address below.
  • Service providers we entrust it to:
    • Google LLC (Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, Gmail; United States): recording what you send, and the notification email to our team.
    • Cloudflare, Inc. (Turnstile; United States): the check against automated submissions. Your IP address and the check token are sent to Cloudflare.

2. How we use your information

  • Providing and improving product features
  • Running AI-powered recommendations and analysis
  • Responding to user support requests
  • Anonymized statistical analysis for service improvement
  • Ensuring security and preventing unauthorized use
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. AI service providers

Our products use the following AI service providers for content generation and analysis:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — text generation, classification, analysis
  • Google (Gemini) — image recognition, text generation

Data sent to AI providers is processed in accordance with their respective privacy policies. Data sent via API is not used for AI model training (per each provider's API terms of service).

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Products that use Google APIs (such as ScaNavi) adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We only use Google user data to provide and improve features described in this policy
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide our service
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising purposes
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless authorized by the user, necessary for security purposes, or required by law

5. Data storage and security

5.1 Storage locations

  • Cloud storage: Supabase (United States), Vercel (global CDN)
  • Temporary processing: AI analysis data is not permanently stored after processing
  • Local processing: Linksee Memory stores data in local SQLite on the user's device (no server transmission)

5.2 Security measures

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • OAuth 2.0 authentication
  • Regular security audits
  • Access controls and monitoring

6. Data sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • AI service providers (as described in Section 3) — only to the extent necessary to provide product features
  • Service providers for the request and consultation form (as described in Section 1.4) — only to the extent necessary to keep records and reply to you
  • Vercel Inc. (Web Analytics) — aggregated page-view statistics only, with no information that identifies you
  • When required by law
  • With your explicit consent

7. Data retention

  • Account data: retained while your account is active
  • Temporary processing data (images, etc.): deleted after processing is complete
  • Usage statistics: retained in anonymized form for service improvement
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke access: Disconnect Google account or social login
  • Data portability: Request an export of your data

9. Children's privacy

Our products are not intended for use by children under 16 years of age. ScaNavi, as an alcohol-related service, is restricted to users aged 20 or older in Japan. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Cookies and tracking

Our websites and web applications use cookies necessary for service delivery. We use privacy-respecting analytics tools and do not engage in tracking that identifies individual users. This website uses cookie-free analytics (Vercel Web Analytics, provided by Vercel Inc., United States) to count page views and summarise referrers, screen sizes and similar data. It does not collect information that identifies you.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through in-product notifications or email. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
