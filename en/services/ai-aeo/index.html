AI映え診断 — paid plans

The stage after the free check has shown that AI gets things wrong about you. We check every wrong statement against your official information, hand you a prioritised fix list and, if you want, do the fixes and measure every month.

Before you pay

Start with the two free stages

Decide on a paid plan only after the free Light check (site readiness) and the free detailed check (three questions on four AIs). If the detailed check finds no wrong statements, you do not need a paid plan. Both are in Japanese; we can walk you through them in English.

PAID 1 — CHECK

Every wrong statement, checked against official information

The free detailed check shows one wrong statement in full. The fact-check report asks 20–30 questions three times each on four AIs and checks every factual claim against your official site and official social accounts.

  • Every wrong statement and what it says
  • The sources each AI relies on
  • Fix list: which site, which field, what value, in what order
PAID 2 — MEASURE

Re-measure after the fixes

AI answers change from day to day, so one answer proves nothing. We repeat the same questions and show the change in wrong statements, mentions and sources in numbers.

  • One-off: once, a month later
  • Monthly: every month, one report and a 30-minute meeting
PAID 3 — FIX

We do the fixes

Beyond the fix list, we correct directory listings, add structured data to your site and rewrite copy.

  • Implementation retainer (monthly)
  • the number of fixes per month is agreed with you
  • One 30-minute online meeting a month
