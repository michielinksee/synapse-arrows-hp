AI VISIBILITY CHECK / AI映え診断

How does AI
describe your business?

We ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity about your shop or company, check every statement against your official information, and show you what to fix.

The free check is in Japanese and built for businesses in Japan.

The free check looks at the directories and listing sites AI relies on in Japan, and its questions and industries are Japanese. That is why we have not translated the tool. If your business is in Japan, you can use it as it is; we can walk you through the result in English. For Singapore and other markets, please talk to us first.

HOW IT WORKS

Two free stages first, then pay only if you need to.

01

Light check

Free, no sign-up

Whether AI can read your site, whether it links to the listing sites AI relies on, and whether your name, address, hours and menu are written in a way machines can read. It does not ask AI anything.

02

Detailed check

Free, email confirmation

Three fixed questions, once each, on four AIs. One wrong statement is shown in full. If no wrong statements are found, you do not need a paid plan.

03

Paid plans

From a one-off report

The fact-check report covers 20–30 questions, three runs each on four AIs. Re-measurement, monthly monitoring and fixes done by us are available after that.

WHAT CHANGES

What changes

Fewer wrong addresses, prices, opening hours and menu items when AI describes you. We do not promise rankings or recommendations; the effect is shown by re-measurement.

Why a minimum term. The listings AI relies on take time to refresh, and seeing the effect of a fix in numbers takes months, so monthly plans run for at least six months.

A dashboard is planned and not yet available. Monthly monitoring is delivered as a report.

A good fit if you

  • notice customers asking AI about your shop or company
  • moved, changed prices or menus, and AI still gives the old information
  • are listed on many directories and do not know which to fix first

Not a fit if you

  • want a guaranteed ranking in a short time
  • want to judge from a single AI answer
  • only want articles produced in bulk

WHO DOES WHAT

Synapse Arrows and KanseiLINK

Consultations, orders and paid work go through Synapse Arrows Pte. Ltd. The check runs on KanseiLINK (mcp.kansei-link.com), a third-party instrument we operate that measures whether AI agents can discover, connect to and use software. KanseiLINK ratings do not change with any contract.
