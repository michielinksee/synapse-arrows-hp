FOR RECIPIENTS

If a research report has reached you

We sometimes send the results of research nobody asked us for. If one has reached you, this is what it is.

  • We look only at information that is already public. We do not handle private, commercial or internal information.
  • We collect by machine, with a pause between requests. We read public pages without putting load on the site.
  • No individual company is named in the report itself. Anything about a single company goes in a separate sheet, and only when that company needs to know it.
  • We do not publish in a form that works against the organisations we looked at. Nothing is published without talking to you first.
  • There is no charge, and no obligation to reply. The report is written to be complete once you have read it.

For questions about the content, or to ask us to drop you from the research, write to contact@synapse-arrows.com.

HOW WE WORK

How we run a study

Every study keeps the same shape.

  • State the scope and the date: how many records, collected when.
  • State the method, in enough detail to be reproduced.
  • Separate what we confirmed from what we could not confirm, and never write the second as the first.
  • Give the limits their own section, so the reader can judge what the result does not mean.
  • Do not write causes or circumstances we did not observe.
